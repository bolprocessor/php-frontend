The default Csound orchestra "0-default.orc" plays piano samples from the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm ([https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html), Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence), and flute samples from the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples ([https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html](https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html)), as does "Beethoven.orc" with its string samples. Details are in the "ReadMe.txt" files of their folders in "csound_resources".
